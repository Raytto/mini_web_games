About this drum kit

Play freely with several fingers. Listen plays three rounds, a fill, and a final crash. Follow the lights waits at every step. For two glowing drums, tap together or hold one finger down while adding the other.

If you tap one at a time, a gentle 700 ms allowance helps combine two hits. There is no timer between steps and no lost progress.

Real drum recordings

Virtuosity Drums — Versilian Studios & Karoryfer Samples; performed by Austin McMahon. CC0 1.0 Universal. Mid microphone recordings, two variants per instrument. Adapted with short pre-roll trimming, level balancing and gentle tail fades. Stored locally as stereo MP3s.

Rhythm examples

Two original beginner patterns: a steady eighth-note beat, and a beat with one extra kick and a quicker tom fill. Both repeat the main bar three times, then play a fourth bar with a fill and a final kick + crash.

Keyboard: Q crash · W high tom · E ride · A hi-hat · S snare · D floor tom · Space kick.
