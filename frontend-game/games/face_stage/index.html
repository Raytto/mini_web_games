Let's get in the picture

  1. A grown-up steadies the device and taps Turn camera on. Allow camera access.
  2. Choose a one-, two- or three-person scene. The two-person scenes start with one big and one little character.
  3. If faces do not fit, open Fit our faces. Drag the circles and adjust their sizes. Swap sides or use the same height.
  4. Make a face, tap the stage for a little surprise, then take a photo. Camera stops after the photo.
  5. Share picture opens your device's menu. Choose Save Image if offered; otherwise download or press and hold the preview. Downloading is not the same as saving to Photos.

No face recognition, recording or microphone. Pictures are combined on this device and are not automatically uploaded. Leaving or switching apps turns the camera off; turn it on again when ready.
